1886 — THE GENESIS

Born in Atlanta

Dr. John S. Pemberton creates the first Coca-Cola syrup at Jacob's Pharmacy. A modest start for a flavor that would eventually bridge cultures across continents.

1915 — THE CONTOUR

A Shape Like No Other

The Root Glass Company creates the "Contour Bottle," designed to be recognized even in the dark or broken on the ground. Identity becomes art.

1971 — GLOBAL HARMONY

I'd Like to Buy the World a Coke

The legendary Hilltop commercial cements the brand's role as a symbol of unity and peace during turbulent times. Coca-Cola becomes more than a drink; it's a message.

PRESENT — DIGITAL FRONTIER

Coca-Cola Creations

Exploring the intersection of the physical and digital. From AI-powered flavors to collaborations with global music icons, we're co-creating the future with our fans.
